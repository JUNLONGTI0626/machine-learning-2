# SKILL: draft-paper

## Purpose
Write paper-ready empirical text from the outputs of the connectedness and ML workflow.

## Use this skill when
- Tables and figures already exist
- You need manuscript text
- Journal-style writing is required

## Inputs
- Tables in `outputs/tables/`
- Figures in `outputs/figures/`
- Reports in `outputs/reports/`

## Outputs
- Draft text for data, methodology, results, and discussion sections

## Steps
1. Read the relevant empirical outputs
2. Summarize only the most important patterns
3. Keep the tone concise and journal-oriented
4. Avoid exaggerated claims
5. Match the writing style to Energy Economics

## Validation checks
- No claim goes beyond the reported evidence
- No repetitive AI-like language
- Frequency-domain and time-domain results are clearly separated
